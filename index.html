How ordering works

From prescription to your door in four steps.

Search or upload

2

Pharmacist checks

The pharmacist on duty reviews the prescription and confirms the medicine, strength and quantity.

3

Packed by batch

4

Delivered and confirmed

A rider brings it in your slot. You confirm with a code, and the order shows as delivered.

Quality and traceability

Know exactly where every pack has been.

Ethiopian medicines carry a GS1 DataMatrix code with the product, batch and expiry. We scan it at every hand-over and report the events to the EFDA-MVC traceability hub.

Questions

Good to know before you order.

Something else? Contact the pharmacy team.

Do I need a prescription?

Only for prescription-only medicines. OTC products such as paracetamol, oral rehydration salts or vitamins can go straight into your cart. For prescription medicines, upload a clear photo and a pharmacist checks it before we pack.

Where do you deliver?

We start in Addis Ababa with same-day slots. Wholesale deliveries run to pharmacies and clinics on scheduled routes, and further cities are added as we grow.

How do I pay?

telebirr, CBE Birr, bank transfer, cards, or cash on delivery. Licensed pharmacies and clinics can apply for 15 or 30-day credit.

How do you make sure medicines are genuine?

We buy only EFDA-registered products from licensed manufacturers and importers, scan each pack's code at receipt, and store and pick by batch and expiry.

How do I open a wholesale account?

Apply from the contact page with your EFDA licence details. Once we verify the licence, you can order in the wholesale portal.
