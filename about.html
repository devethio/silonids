About Silondis

Medicines people can trust, from the warehouse to the bedside.

Silondis is an Ethiopian pharmacy and pharmaceutical wholesaler. We supply patients directly, and we supply the pharmacies, drug shops and clinics that care for their communities.

Every pack we sell can be traced to its batch, its expiry and its source.

Finding the right medicine in Ethiopia often means calling or visiting several pharmacies, and not always knowing whether a pack is genuine or close to expiry. Pharmacies face the same problem one step up the chain: stock-outs, unclear prices and short-dated deliveries.

Silondis brings retail and wholesale onto one licensed stock and one system. A patient ordering online and a clinic reordering in bulk see the same live stock, packed by batch and checked by pharmacists.

What we do

Three services, one standard.

Retail pharmacy

For patients and families in Addis Ababa.

  • Online shop in Amharic and English
  • Pharmacist-checked prescriptions
  • Same-day delivery or branch pick-up
  • Refill reminders for chronic care

Wholesale supply

For licensed pharmacies, drug shops and clinics.

  • Online reorder portal with live stock
  • Tiered prices and credit terms
  • Scheduled delivery runs
  • Short-expiry deals, clearly marked

Institutions and tenders

For hospitals, NGOs and public buyers.

  • Bids through the national eGP portal
  • Contract delivery schedules
  • Cold-chain handling
  • Batch records for every shipment
What we stand for

Four commitments behind every order.

EFDA-registered products from licensed sources, scanned at every hand-over.

ተደራሽነትAccessible

ተጠያቂነትAccountable
